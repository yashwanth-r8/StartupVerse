HOW TO READ PATENT DOCUMENTS

Application, publication, grant: what's the difference?

A1 / A publications

Usually identify a published application. Publication alone does not mean the claims were granted.

B / B2 grant documents

Identify granted-patent documents in many patent systems. Confirm the current legal status and maintenance events with the official register.

WO / PCT publications

Published through the international PCT system. A PCT publication is not a single “world patent”; national or regional phases are separate.

Priority vs. filing vs. publication

Priority is an earliest claimed filing date, filing is the application date, and publication is when the document became public. These dates can differ substantially.
